3. **Credential/session actions:** as relevant, rotate MongoDB Atlas, Vercel, admin and API credentials; invalidate compromised sessions; restrict database/network access; contact Vercel and MongoDB Atlas through verified emergency channels. Do not rely on a backup restore path: Atlas currently shows **Backups Inactive**.

Have the legal/privacy reviewer apply then-current Malaysian PDPA and Commissioner guidance to decide Commissioner and affected-person notification, channel, content and timing. Record rationale and staged updates.
5. **Recovery and review:** verify containment, restore safely, reapply pending deletion requests after restore, monitor recurrence, document root cause and corrective work, and test the runbook after material architecture changes.
