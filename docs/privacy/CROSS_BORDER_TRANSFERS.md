## Section 129 candidate condition

**Candidate:** paragraph 129(3)(f), reasonable precautions and due diligence. **Status:** `Operator assessment: provisional; external legal review pending.` Red Island Studio treats this as the candidate for routine cloud-provider transfers. The [Commissioner's guideline](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/GP_CBPDT_EN-1.pdf) says the controller may refer to this paragraph only after taking all reasonable precautions and exercising all due diligence to avoid overseas processing that would contravene Act 709 if done in Malaysia. It identifies binding corporate rules, contractual clauses and recognised certification as mechanisms; contractual clauses should provide protection at least equivalent to Act 709 and guarantee compliant processing. Receiver compliance must continue to be monitored. This assessment does not establish that every condition has been met.

Existing technical and organisational safeguards: HTTPS/provider transport security; Production-only Vercel Secrets for `MONGODB_URI` and `CRON_SECRET`; production/Preview database isolation; exact-bearer authentication and missing-database fail-closed behavior for the retention route; account/role/admin access controls; public highscore API minimisation; account deletion of profile/progress, sessions, account-linked scores and raw activity; bounded, idempotent 90-day activity cleanup prepared in source without long-term derived per-learner behavioural profiles. The cleanup is **not yet deployed or active**, so live 90-day storage enforcement is pending. The bilingual `/privacy` Privacy Notice is the written notice informing learners of MongoDB Atlas storage in Singapore and Vercel processing that may occur outside Malaysia, including the United States. It does not describe public scores as anonymous. No cross-border consent flow is proposed or implemented as a workaround.
